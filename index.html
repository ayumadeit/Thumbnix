/ faq

Got questions?
Our buddy has answers.

Quick answers about downloads, sizes and rights.

What is Thumbnix?

A tiny free tool that fetches the thumbnail images YouTube already publishes for a video, so you can preview and save them.

How do I download a YouTube thumbnail?

Paste the video link, hit Get Thumbnail, pick a resolution, and press Download.

Can I download HD thumbnails?

Yes, when the video has one. Thumbnix checks which sizes really exist, and shows only those.

Does it support YouTube Shorts?

Yes. watch, youtu.be, shorts, embed and live links all work.

Is Thumbnix free?

Completely free.

Do I need to sign up?

Nope. No accounts, no tracking forms.

Can I use downloaded thumbnails commercially?

Not automatically. Downloading a thumbnail doesn't give you ownership or reuse rights. The image belongs to the video's creator, so get their permission before reusing it.
